    EGG WEIGHT LOSS ANALYSER · THE HATCH PREDICTOR
    AWAITING INPUT
    Weigh the egg the day it is laid, then every 3 days. This tells you if your humidity is right for THESE eggs, in THIS room. It is the only true measurement. A wall sensor guesses; the egg does not.
    TARGET: lovebird eggs must lose 13-16% of fresh weight by day 21 (internal pip), about 0.69%/day. Losing too fast = air too dry, RAISE humidity. Too slow = air too wet, LOWER humidity. Inadequate weight loss drowns fully-formed chicks at internal pip, the exact dead-in-shell you are fighting.

      SCIENCE CORE · VERIFIED PARAMETERS
      PHOTOPERIOD = YOUR WEAPON
      14-16h

      Increasing day length is the strongest breeding trigger in birds. 12h = maintenance. 14-16h = breeding stimulation. 8-10h = shuts hormones down. You control this with smart lights. Competitors on natural light cannot.

      Photoperiod control of avian reproduction, Chewy / Animal Start
      EGG WEIGHT LOSS
      13-16%

      Loss from lay to internal pip. The single ground-truth measure of whether humidity is correct. Too little loss = chick drowns at internal pip. Too much = shrink-wrapped membrane.

      Hagen Avicultural Research Institute (HARI)
      FULL-TERM DEAD-IN-SHELL
      HUMIDITY

      Late-stage deaths are most often humidity-related, usually too high. Fully formed embryos can drown in the shell at internal pip due to inadequate egg weight loss. Fix the water, not the heat.

      Uneek Poultry incubation science; HARI
      VITAMIN E + SELENIUM
      DAY 3-4

      Deficiency kills embryos early (day 3-4) via defective blood vessel formation, and drops male fertility. Selenium is required for vitamin E absorption. Dose both, never E alone.

      Merck Veterinary Manual; Wikifarmer poultry nutrition
      HEN CALCIUM / REST
      2-3 mo

      Hens need 2-3 months between clutches to rebuild calcium. Back-to-back cycling causes depletion, egg-binding, and rising dead-in-shell. Rotate cohorts instead: the aviary produces all year, no single hen burns out.

      Lafeber Pet Birds, lovebird breeding
      THE OCTOBER PROOF
      33°C / 76%

      Dhaka October averages 33°C max and 76% RH. That is your best breeding month on record (4/5, 5/5 clutches). Conclusion: lovebirds breed excellently at 75% humidity. Humidity is not your blocker.

      Foreca / World Weather & Climate 1990-2020 normals

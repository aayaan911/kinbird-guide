    AWAITING INPUT
    Weigh each egg on lay day, then every 3 days. Enter the numbers and this tells you exactly which direction to move your humidity.
    TARGET: lovebird eggs must lose 13–16% of fresh weight by day 21 (internal pip), about 0.69% per day. Too fast means the air is too dry — raise humidity. Too slow means too wet — lower it. Inadequate weight loss drowns fully formed chicks at internal pip, exactly the dead-in-shell you are fighting.

          PHOTOPERIOD — YOUR WEAPON
          14–16h

          Increasing day length is the strongest breeding trigger in birds. 12h is maintenance, 14–16h stimulates breeding, 8–10h shuts hormones down. You control this with smart lights. Competitors on natural daylight cannot.

          Avian photoperiod control literature
          EGG WEIGHT LOSS
          13–16%

          Loss from lay day to internal pip. The ground truth for correct humidity. Too little and the chick drowns at internal pip; too much and the membrane shrink-wraps it.

          Hagen Avicultural Research Institute (HARI)
          FULL-TERM DEAD-IN-SHELL
          HUMIDITY

          Late-stage deaths are most often humidity related, and usually too high. Fully formed embryos drown inside the shell at internal pip when weight loss was inadequate. Fix the water, not the heat.

          Uneek Poultry incubation science; HARI
          VITAMIN E + SELENIUM
          DAY 3–4

          Deficiency kills embryos early, around day 3–4, through defective blood vessel formation, and sharply reduces male fertility. Selenium is required for vitamin E absorption — never dose E alone.

          Merck Veterinary Manual; Wikifarmer
          HEN CALCIUM & REST
          2–3 mo

          Hens need two to three months between clutches to rebuild calcium. Back-to-back cycling causes depletion, egg binding and rising dead-in-shell. Rotate cohorts: the aviary produces all year while no hen burns out.

          Lafeber Pet Birds — lovebird breeding
          THE OCTOBER PROOF
          33°C / 76%

          Dhaka's October normals are 33°C max and 76% RH — and that is your best breeding month on record. Conclusion: lovebirds breed excellently at 75% humidity. Humidity is not your blocker.

          Foreca / World Weather & Climate, 1990–2020
